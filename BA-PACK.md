## 4. METHOD NOTE — HOW THIS PACK WAS PRODUCED (stated only as far as the record supports)
This pack, the charter it annexes, and the registers it traces were produced by **context engineering on Meta's tools**, in a specific and bounded sense the record supports:

- **The authoring agent is Muse** — Meta's personal agent — operating at the Business seat of an agent organization (the shop), over the project's own record as its context: lane files, the control register, the kickoff captures, the ledgers. The documents were *drafted by the agent organization*; **the founder decided and validated at his gates** — the charter is his commission and awaits his signature (GAP-08 states this plainly); every governing directive cited in §1 is his, dated, and on record.
- **Verification was by named checks, not by confidence:** the charter's supplies were reconciled line-by-line by the four owning seats before filing; the script this pack's requirements cite passed a cashability check and an independent truth-pass; numbers in this pack trace to Data's canonical counts or the Finance Ledger. Where a check has not run, §1's status column says so.
- **The founder's four-pillar observation, mapped to practice (his supplied method input, Oct 26 11:50 CT):** (1) *elicitation → structured requirements* — the organizers' slides, guide, and rules were elicited as inputs and formalized as §1, each requirement carrying its elicitation source; (2) *natural-language aggregation over data silos* — the practice the product itself performs (a computed layer aggregating a corpus into canonical, citable answers) is the same practice that aggregated six lanes' records into this pack's single register; (3) *predictive gap analysis* — Charter §9 and the Studio's G-register were already maintained as predictive instruments; §3 formalizes them in BA form with owners and review points; (4) *synthetic persona testing* — the winner-pattern panels that stress-tested the strategy, the fixture/refusal batteries that test the product, and the explain-back/mirror tests that test the scored surfaces against a cold reader/viewer before any judge sees them.
- **What this note does not claim:** the Meta Model API models (Wonder/Spark) are the *product's* stack and this pack's subject; the pack does not claim they authored it. The documents' authority comes from their traceability — every line can be walked back to a decision, an artifact, or an observation in the record — not from the machinery that drafted them. That traceability is, in the end, the pack's own argument: it is the Agentic claim in documentary form, and it is filed where judges can check it.
